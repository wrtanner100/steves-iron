FAQ

Good questions, straight answers

Still unsure? Call (949) 456-0176 and ask Steve.

Do you really come to me?

Yes. Our mobile welding service comes to your business, home, garage, dock or roadside location. We bring everything needed, including a generator to power our electric welders.

Can I bring something to you instead?

Yes. If you bring materials to us, welding has a $95 minimum.

Are you licensed?

Yes. Steve's Iron is a California licensed C-23 Ornamental Metals Welding Contractor, License #968768.

Do you handle emergencies?

Yes, 24/7. Emergency fence repairs, gates that won't close or lock, broken security bars: call and we'll get out to you.
